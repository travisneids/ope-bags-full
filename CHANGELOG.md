# Changelog

## v0.1.1

- First release on Wago Addons. No changes in game since v0.1.0.

## v0.1.0

- First release for WoW Forever (beta) and Classic Era / Hardcore.
- Make room: combine partial stacks, then get rid of the cheapest junk one
  click at a time until you reach your free-slot goal.
- A bag icon beside your backpack shows your free slots. Shift-click it, use a
  key binding or `/ope trash` to trash the cheapest item.
- Sells junk automatically at vendors.
- Alerts you when you loot with full bags and names what to trash.
- Optional: let white or green items go too; keep and junk lists; keeps
  crafting materials your professions use if Stash or Trash is installed.
